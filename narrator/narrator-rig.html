These are the same 4 poses the render pipeline cycles through once per sentence (steady/jolt are the small line-weight flicker, lean_left/lean_right add the bent-elbow arm, leg stance shift, and body tilt). Picking one here freezes the idle sway/blink/wobble animations so the pose shows cleanly -- use "Resume idle animation" to go back to the normal live-preview loop.

Auto‑talk cycles the three talking mouths on a random clock, the way the reference channel's frames never quite line up with the words, and now occasionally gives the brows a small lift on a wide-open frame too. Auto‑blink fires every 2.5–5s, and roughly every third blink also raises the brows for a bigger beat. Separately, the eyes glance left or right for a second or two every ~2.2–4.5s on their own independent clock, then return to center -- same flat pupil, just shifted, not a different eye shape -- so the character isn't staring dead‑center the whole video. A blink can interrupt a glance and resumes it afterward instead of snapping back to center. The head itself has a slow idle bob/tilt so it isn't frozen while the arms sway. Wobble redraws the turbulence seed a few times a second for the slight hand‑jitter on the outline.

No beard, plain neat hair, glasses. Body squared off (straight sides instead of the curvy bulge), legs shortened, and both arms back to hanging symmetrically with open hands -- matching a plain reference figure with just a shirt added.
