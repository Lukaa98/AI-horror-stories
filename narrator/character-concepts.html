Narrator Character Concepts

Four car/mechanical mascot directions, side by side for a quick gut-check before committing to one. Same line-art language (white fill, dark stroke) as the current human rig, just swapping what the "face" is built from.

Concept 1

Car-Front Face

Headlights = eyes, grille slats = a mouth that literally parts like teeth. Grille-teeth map straight onto the existing open/closed/talking mouth states. Whole silhouette reads as "car" immediately.

Concept 2 — recommended

Steering Wheel Head

Wheel rim + horn pad = head and face; 3 spokes read as expressive brow lines. Body/arms/legs stay exactly the existing human rig, so all current pose/mouth/blink work carries over untouched -- smallest rework, still a big novelty jump.

Concept 3

Gauge Cluster Head

Dial face + tick marks, needle doubles as a swinging brow/emphasis pointer, a tiny secondary dial stands in for a mouth. Most unique of the four ("the narrator IS the stat readout"), but the needle only really does one continuous gesture -- harder to get discrete talking/expression states out of it.

Concept 4

Bolt-Head Buddy

Hex bolt head + held wrench prop -- a genuine garage-toolbox mascot, and the one spot where hard angular facets actually belong (a real bolt has flat faces). Reads more "generic mechanic" than "car review narrator" specifically, though.
